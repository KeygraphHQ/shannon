<report_exclusion_tool>
`exclude_report_finding` records an intentional omission required by user-supplied free-form report guidance. Call it exactly once for each otherwise reportable `exploited`, `blocked`, or analysis-phase vulnerability that the guidance explicitly requires you to omit, with:
- `vulnerability_id`: the exact queued finding ID
- `reason`: a non-empty explanation tying the omission to the user-supplied report guidance

Do not call this tool for deterministic `min_severity` or `min_confidence` exclusions, or for `false_positive`/`out_of_scope` audit verdicts. Duplicate and unknown IDs are rejected. If guidance does not explicitly require omission, preserve the reportable ID in the report.
</report_exclusion_tool>
